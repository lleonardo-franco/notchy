# Code of Conduct

Notchy wants to be a friendly place to learn, build and share, for people of every background and experience level. This applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other space connected to the project, in any language.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people contributing for the first time.
- Assume good intent and ask before assuming the worst.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person, and be specific about how to improve it.
- Accept feedback gracefully and change your mind when you learn something new.
- Respect people's time: keep discussions on topic and search before asking.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, slurs or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language about anyone's identity, background or abilities.
- Sexualized language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing conversations, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Any behavior that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may temporarily or permanently block people from the project, depending on how serious and how repeated the behavior is.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer [@lleonardo-franco](https://github.com/lleonardo-franco) privately through the contact details on their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.
